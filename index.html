About

I'm an Oracle Cloud consultant at Novamodus Solutions, where I have worked since August 2025 on General Ledger, Budgetary Control, Cash Management and EPM Planning for public-sector and healthcare clients. The work is hands-on: configuring financial controls and journal approvals, writing EPM calculation rules, running UAT, writing functional specifications, and tracking down problems in production.

Deployed to production

Multi-year salary and insurance planning in EPM

Calculation rules that project salary, benefit and insurance expense year over year through FY2032.

EPM Planning, Calculation Manager, Essbase calculation functions, runtime prompts, Smart View

Read the case study

Business challenge

Finance needed salary, benefit and insurance expense projected at different annual rates across a multi-year horizon, by account group.

My responsibilities

Developed and modified the calculation rules, tested them and deployed them to production.

Technical approach

  • A 2% compounding annual increase on selected salary and benefit accounts, and 7% on selected insurance accounts.
  • Extended the calculations through FY2032.
  • Data copy logic using DATACOPY with runtime prompts for source and target years, scenario and version.
  • Level-0 member selection to keep calculation scope tight, and fixes for scope and performance issues.
  • Worked on the existing CPI-based and trend-based forecast calculations and prior-year assumptions.

Testing and validation

Checked results in planning forms and Smart View, and supported business users through Financial Planning UAT.

Outcome

The salary and insurance rules are in production.

General Ledger financial controls

Journal approvals, account combination rules and journal entry controls for a public-sector utility.

Oracle Fusion General Ledger, cross-validation rules, journal approval rules, spreadsheet journals

Read the case study

Business challenge

Stop invalid account combinations at entry, route journals to the right approvers by amount and team, and improve the quality of journal data.

My responsibilities

Configured and tested the controls, and wrote the functional specifications for page changes.

Technical approach

  • Cross-validation rules across Department, Service and Natural Account segments.
  • Journal approval rules with monetary thresholds, supervisory and position-based hierarchies, and separate routing for Controllership and FP&A.
  • Specifications for mandatory journal descriptions and for restricting AutoCopy on subledger journals.

Testing and validation

UAT scenarios covering journal submission and approval, spreadsheet uploads, reversals, revaluation and funds checking. Some scenarios found issues that needed investigation before sign-off.

Current status

Approval and cross-validation rules are configured and tested. The page customizations are specified and with the client for sign-off.

Issues resolved

ERP to EPM integration troubleshooting

Finding out why financial and budget data failed to load between Oracle Fusion ERP and EPM Planning.

EPM Data Integration, Data Management, Budgetary Control, Smart View

Read the case study

Business challenge

Budget and actuals loads were failing or arriving incomplete, and finance needed to trust the numbers in both systems.

My responsibilities

Investigated the failures, reviewed the mappings and worked with the technical and functional teams on fixes.

Technical approach

  • Read Data Integration job logs step by step to find whether a failure was in the extract, the mapping or the target load.
  • Reviewed dimension, scenario, category and period mappings, and traced unknown members and invalid combinations back to their source.
  • Investigated drill-through from reported numbers back to source financial data.

Testing and validation

Reconciled loaded data against the source after each fix.

Outcome

The failures were traced to their causes and fixed with the project teams.

Proposed design

Replacement dispatch in Oracle Field Service

Assessing whether Oracle Field Service can dispatch replacement staff for a school district, and designing for the gaps.

Oracle Field Service, CX Service, Oracle Integration Cloud (proposed)

Read the case study

Business challenge

Assign replacement staff for absences using qualifications, seniority, availability and preferences, with approvals before an assignment is confirmed.

My responsibilities

Requirements analysis, fit-gap, proof-of-concept configuration and the proposed solution design.

Technical approach

  • Tested standard scheduling and routing against the district's rules to find what configuration alone could handle.
  • For the gaps, drafted pseudocode and an Oracle Integration Cloud design that routes requests through approval before pushing confirmed work to Field Service.

Current status

The proof of concept won sign-off to move to CRP 1. The integration design is proposed and not built.
